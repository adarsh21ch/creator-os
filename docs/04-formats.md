# Format library

**Sample: 4 winners, 3 losers (2026-09-16).** Small, and one confound noted at the bottom.
Promotion rule: a pattern becomes a Format only if it appears in **3+ winners AND is
meaningfully rarer in the losers**.

---

## KILLED — patterns that failed the losers test

These were promoted from winners-only analysis on 2026-09-16 and **disproved the same day** once
losing transcripts arrived. Recorded here so nobody re-derives them.

### ~~H-01 · The Counted Zero~~ — NOT a cause of winning
The guess-the-number device (`ab aap guess karlo` → walk the number down → the real figure).
Present in winners w01 and w03. **Also present in loser l01**, which used it almost identically
(`Ab aap guess kar sakte ho NGO kitne register hue honge? 6 lakh...`) and still took 5–6k views
*with paid promotion*.
**Conclusion:** the counting is delivery, not cause. w03 won because AAP broke a countable
promise, not because the number was counted down.

### ~~H-02 · Their Words, Their Weapon~~ — NOT a cause of winning
State the opponent's claim, then flatly contradict it. Present in winners w01, w02, w04.
**Also present in loser l03** (`Kai logon ko lagta hai... Nahi bhai`), which is the only
*organic* loser in the sample and therefore the cleanest comparison available.
**Conclusion:** this is his default rhetorical habit — it shows up whether he wins or loses.
It is a **voice marker**, and belongs in `03-voice-profile.md`, not in the format library.

## F-01 · Named Living Villain — PROMOTED
**4 of 4 winners. 0 of 3 losers.** The strongest signal in the sample.

The reel is aimed at a **specific, living, recognizable target the audience already has feelings
about** — a named party or leader, a visible group of creators or celebrities, or people the
viewer argues with in real life.

| Reel | Target | Result |
|---|---|---|
| w01 | left-wing creators, celebrities, influencers | WIN |
| w02 | the protestors and their stage slogans | WIN |
| w03 | AAP / Bhagwant Mann | WIN |
| w04 | people claiming reservation is a fundamental right | WIN |
| l01 | "NGOs" — an abstract category, 6 lakh faceless entities | LOSS |
| l02 | nobody until the final 5 seconds | LOSS |
| l03 | Marx, Lenin, Mao — dead foreign ideologues | LOSS |

**Rule for C-03 Scriptwriter:** before writing, name the target out loud. If it is an
institution, a statistic, a law, or a historical figure, the script has no villain yet —
find the living person or group who is doing this *now*, or reject the topic.

## F-02 · Stake On The Viewer — PROMOTED
**4 of 4 winners. 0 of 3 losers.**

Winners hand the viewer a **grievance that touches their own life**: their exam, their child's
admission, their job, their country being insulted, a promise broken to them. Losers hand the
viewer **information** — a policy mechanism, a good-news update, an ideology explainer.

- w02 and w04: reservation — directly touches the viewer's exam and career
- w01: national insult — personal pride
- w03: a promise broken to voters — betrayal
- l01: FCRA regulation — structurally interesting, personally distant
- l02: **positive news with no grievance at all** — nothing to be angry about, nothing at stake
- l03: intellectual history

**Rule for C-03 Scriptwriter:** every script must be able to answer *"what has been taken from
the viewer, or is about to be?"* in one sentence. If the answer is "nothing, but it is
important to know" — that is an l01/l02, and it will die.

## Supporting signals — not promoted, watch for confirmation

- **Runtime.** The two longest reels in the sample (l01 at ~2:45, l03 at ~2:13) both lost. But
  w04 ran ~1:50 and won, so length alone is not fatal. Likely a symptom of the explainer mode
  rather than a cause. Needs more data.
- **Proper-noun load.** l01 carries DPIIT, FCRA, Kudankulam, Income Tax Department, Supreme
  Court, the Odisha coal project, an IB report, and six separate figures. Winners carry far
  fewer: w03's entire numeric content is *16 promised, 0 built*. Hypothesis: **one number beats
  six.** Only 2 winners use numbers at all, so it cannot be promoted yet.
- **Asking for patience.** l01 says `aap video aakhri tak dekhna` at 0:04. n=1 in this sample,
  so this is **general short-form principle, not a finding from his data** — flagged as a rule
  to test, not a proven one.
- **Dead endings.** l01 ends on housekeeping (`me description aur comment section me isko pin kar
  dunga`). l03 ends passively (`ab aapke upar hai`). Every winner ends on a sting aimed at
  someone. Worth testing as a rule.

## Confound — read before trusting the above

**l01 and l02 were paid promotions**; the winners were organic. Promoted reels reach people who
do not know him, and a cold audience behaves differently from a warm one — so part of that gap
may be distribution rather than content quality.

**l03 is the exception and carries the most weight:** organic, under 10k, and it still fits
both F-01 and F-02 cleanly. That is what keeps the two promoted formats standing.

**Next validation step:** 3–5 more *organic* losers. If F-01 and F-02 hold there, they are real.
