## H-01 · The Counted Zero
*Seen in transcripts 01 and 03.*

| Beat | Timing |
|---|---|
| Mock-praise or neutral setup | 0–5s |
| The promise they made | 5–15s |
| "Ab aap guess karlo" — invite the audience in | 15–25s |
| Walk the number down: 100%? 50%? 4? 2? ek to? | 25–35s |
| **"Zero."** as its own line | 35–40s |
| Turn their own positioning into the closing question | 40–55s |

**Mechanism:** the audience commits to a guess, so the real number lands as a personal surprise
rather than a fact they were told. The countdown makes them wrong repeatedly before the reveal.

**Topic-free** — works on any promise by any party, and on business/startup claims.

## H-02 · Their Words, Their Weapon
*Seen in transcripts 01, 02 and 04.*

| Beat | Timing |
|---|---|
| Their strongest claim or slogan, verbatim | 0–6s |
| Flat contradiction, one line | 6–10s |
| Explain the real mechanism calmly, with a legal or factual specific | 10–40s |
| Produce the moment they said the opposite | 40–60s |
| Sting — name who is harmed | 60–75s |

**Mechanism:** opening with the opposition's own words makes the viewer feel represented before
they feel attacked, so the contradiction lands as correction rather than as insult. The calm
middle section buys credibility for the sharp ending.
