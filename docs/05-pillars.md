# Pillars (topic territories)

These replace the pillars in `content_reel_system.md`, which belong to a different account.

1. **Accountability** — a promise made vs what actually exists. Party-agnostic by design.
2. **Reservation & social policy** — his recurring heavy topic.
3. **Narrative war** — how India gets framed abroad, and who stays silent about it.
4. **Explainer** — what a term, law or ruling actually says, against what people assume.
5. **Business politics** — office, corporate and startup politics. His crossover, and the only
   pillar that reaches beyond the political audience.

**Pillars are topics. Formats are structures.** Keeping them separate is what lets the same
format be fired at any pillar. See `docs/04-formats.md`.
