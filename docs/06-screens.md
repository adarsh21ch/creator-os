# Screens

Sidebar navigation, responsive to phone width. Twelve screens in four groups.

**Daily**
- **Today** — the 06:00 brief. The only screen he needs most mornings.
- **Ideas** — ranked topic menu, approve or reject.
- **Studio** — the writing room. Pick a topic → hooks → script → packaging, rewrite by talking.
- **Board** — Kanban, Idea through Published.

**What you learn**
- **Formats** — template library, Proven and Candidate, with use counts and fatigue trends.
- **Library** — every reel ever posted: transcript, metrics, format used. *The foundation table.*
- **Performance** — follows, saves, profile visits, broken down.

**What you feed it**
- **Watchlist** — 40 accounts, filter by wing / topic / date.
- **Sources** — newspapers and RSS feeds.
- **Brand Brain** — voice profile, pillars, hook formula, banned claims, language register.

**System**
- **Employees** — all 13 with prompts, models, schedules, monthly spend. Edit or disable any.
- **Settings** — Anthropic / Gemini switch, Instagram connection.
