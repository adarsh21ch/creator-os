# Voice profile — seed version

Derived from 4 transcripts in `data/transcripts/`, all **confirmed winners** (he stated 2026-09-16
that every reel he sent performed well). This is a seed. Replace it with a profile built from

## His six beats, in order

1. **Open by stating the other side's position, in their own words** — their actual slogan, the
   photo everyone has seen, or mock-praise. He never opens with his own claim.
2. **Contradict it flatly and early, in one short line.** No hedging.
3. **Prove it with something countable** — most often an *absence*. Zero left-wing creators. Zero
   medical colleges.
4. **Repeat a line for emphasis.**
5. **Turn their own words back on them.** Present in all four transcripts — his single most-used
   device. What they said then, against what they are doing now.
6. **Close on a sting** — a rhetorical question, a Hindi idiom, or naming who gets hurt.

## Voice markers

- **Teacher framing:** "pehle baat ye samjho", "main aapko samjha deta hu"
- **Direct address:** "aap mujhe batao", "mere bhai", "guess karlo"
- **Pre-emptive neutrality shield:** "main kisi political party ki baat hi nahi karunga" — disarms
  the audience before a partisan argument
- **Speaks as "hum", not "main"** — positions himself inside a group, not as a lone commentator
- **Rhythm:** long build-up, then a one-word line. "Nahi." "Zero! Sannata." This is the most
  distinctive thing he does and the Scriptwriter must reproduce it.
- **Authority moves:** constitutional and legal specifics, dates, counts
- **Hindi idioms as closers**
- **Occasional crude Hindi word** (transcript 02). He wants brand deals; Brand Brain needs a
  **language register** setting and he must choose where it sits. Decision pending.

## Writing instructions for C-03 Scriptwriter

- Hinglish, Roman script, casual-argumentative register
- 60–90 seconds, talking head
- Never open with his own thesis — open with theirs
- Put the flat contradiction before the 10-second mark
- Find the countable proof; prefer an absence over a statistic
- Locate the "they said the opposite" moment — if none exists, say so rather than inventing one
- End on a question or a named victim, not on a summary
